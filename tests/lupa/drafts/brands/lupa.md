# Brand: Lupa

## Purpose

Lupa is a private banking interface that gives clients clear, confident control over their finances. Every surface communicates competence and discretion, letting the numbers speak while the interface stays out of the way.

## Voice

Professional, concise, and trustworthy. Lupa speaks like a private banker: direct without being curt, informative without being verbose. Labels state facts. Confirmations reassure. Errors explain what happened and what to do next, never blame the user. Currency amounts always show two decimal places and use locale-appropriate grouping.

## Visual Identity

### Colours

- surface-neutral: the default background across all views, a muted warm off-white that reduces glare during extended use
- surface-elevated: a slightly lighter tone used for cards, modals, and panels that sit above the neutral surface
- text-primary: near-black used for balances, headings, and any content requiring immediate attention
- text-secondary: a softer mid-tone for labels, timestamps, and supporting copy
- accent-action: a deep blue conveying reliability, used for primary buttons and interactive links
- accent-warning: a muted amber reserved for alerts that require attention but not urgency
- accent-destructive: a restrained red for irreversible actions and error states
- positive-delta: a subdued green indicating credits and favourable balance changes
- negative-delta: a muted red indicating debits and unfavourable balance changes

### Typography

- display: used sparingly for the largest balances on summary screens, high contrast and generous line height
- heading: section titles and card headers, medium weight, slightly tighter line height than body
- body: default reading text for descriptions, messages, and form labels, optimised for legibility at moderate sizes
- body-emphasis: same size as body but heavier weight, used for inline highlights such as counterparty names and amounts within prose
- mono-numeric: a tabular-figure face used wherever columns of numbers must align, including balances, transaction amounts, and account numbers

### Spacing

- tight: minimal breathing room between tightly related elements such as a label and its value
- compact: comfortable spacing within a component, between rows in a list, or between form field and its helper text
- default: standard separation between sibling components on a page
- loose: generous padding around major content blocks to create visual breathing room
- section: the largest gap, used to separate distinct page sections or before a page footer

## Motion

- instant: state changes that must feel immediate, such as button press feedback and toggle flips
- quick: transitions that orient the user without slowing them down, such as card expansion and dropdown appearance
- considered: deliberate animations for significant state changes like screen transitions, modal entry, and page-level loading states

## Imagery

Photography is never decorative. The only images in Lupa are user-supplied profile photos and institutional logos retrieved from payment networks. All imagery displays at a fixed aspect ratio with a neutral fallback when unavailable.

## Iconography

Icons are line-style, single-weight, and monochrome, tinted to text-secondary by default and to accent-action when interactive. Every icon carries an accessible label. The set covers: navigation arrows, account types, transaction categories, status indicators, and chat actions.

## Logo

The Lupa wordmark appears in text-primary on surface-neutral in the top navigation bar. It is always displayed at heading scale and never combined with a symbol or tagline. On compact viewports the wordmark may abbreviate to its initial letter, retaining the same colour and weight.
